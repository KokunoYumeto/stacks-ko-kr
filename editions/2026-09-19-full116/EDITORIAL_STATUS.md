# Editorial status

This preservation edition contains all 116 chapters. Complete coverage does not
mean completed register normalization or an error-free scholarly translation.
Known terminology and sentence-structure issues remain under correction.

For example, the big/small sites discussion in `src/etale-cohomology.tex`, near
the labels `etale-cohomology-definition-tau-covering` and
`etale-cohomology-definition-standard-tau`, contains wording that needs renewed
source-and-Korean-register comparison, including `구문론적` for syntomic and
`접근하기 어려운 추기경` in the inaccessible-cardinal discussion. Mechanical
formula and identifier parity does not establish the correctness of that prose.

The present release preserves the current complete translation with explicit
qualifications; it does not claim every translation choice is canon-attested or
expert-certified. The original English source remains the mathematical authority.
Documented assembly and clipping repairs do not silently normalize the text.
